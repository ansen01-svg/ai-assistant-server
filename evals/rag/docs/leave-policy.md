# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly.
Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days of sick leave.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.
