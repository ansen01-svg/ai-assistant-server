# New Hire Onboarding

## Before day one

Your laptop is shipped 3 days before your start date. Accounts are activated on your first morning.

## First week

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from their team for the first month.

## Probation

New employees have a probation period of 6 months, with check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget for courses, books and conferences.
