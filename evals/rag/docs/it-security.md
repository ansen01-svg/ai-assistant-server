# IT Security Guidelines

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager; never reuse passwords.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is mandatory for email, the HR portal and all production systems.

## Phishing

Forward suspicious emails to security@northwind.example and do not click any links in them.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT within 1 hour so it can be remotely wiped. All laptops use full-disk encryption.
