# Travel & Expense Policy

## Meals

While travelling for work, meals are reimbursed up to ₹1,500 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Flights

Book economy class by default. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims with itemised receipts within 30 days of the expense. Late claims need finance approval.

## Approvals

Any single expense above ₹20,000 needs written approval from your manager before booking.
